# Trademark Policy

NoWomp's code and artwork are open source under the [AGPLv3](LICENSE). You're free to fork, modify and redistribute them under that license.

The **name "NoWomp"** and the **app icon** (the penguin holding dumbbells in `assets/images/icon.png`) identify the official app. To avoid confusing people about who made what:

## Please don't

- publish a modified version under the name "NoWomp" or a confusingly similar name;
- use the app icon as the icon of a modified version;
- suggest that your version is official or endorsed by the NoWomp project.

## You're welcome to

- say your app is "based on NoWomp" or "a fork of NoWomp";
- use the name to refer to the project, for example in articles, reviews and tutorials;
- keep using the in-app pixel-art penguin and trophies, which are part of the open-source artwork under the AGPLv3;
- build and run unmodified official releases however you like.

If you're unsure whether something is OK, ask: developernowomp@gmail.com
